# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, constructive space for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and collaborative.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Respect privacy and do not share credentials, private media, or personal data.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive conduct or bad-faith security disclosures.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards. Report concerns privately through the repository's security reporting channel.
